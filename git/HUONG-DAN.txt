HUONG DAN CHUAN BI ANH MINH CHUNG GITHUB/GITLAB
================================================

MUC DICH

Thu muc nay dung de nop anh chup that ve qua trinh lam viec nhom tren repo.
Anh can chung minh du an dien ra it nhat 1 thang, moi thanh vien co it nhat
2 commit moi tuan, dong gop can bang va co hop tac chu dong. Khong chup anh
gia lap hoac chi chup mot trang README de thay cho lich su lam viec.

CAC ANH CAN CHUP

1. 01-repository.png
   Trang repo GitHub/GitLab, thay ten repo va danh sach thanh vien/organization
   neu nen tang hien thi thong tin nay.

2. 02-contributors.png
   Trang Contributors/Insights (GitHub) hoac trang dong gop tuong ung (GitLab).
   Can thay ten tai khoan va phan bo dong gop cua tung thanh vien.

3. 03-commit-history-start.png va 04-commit-history-end.png
   Lich su commit o giai doan dau va giai doan gan hien tai. De chung minh khoang
   thoi gian it nhat 1 thang, anh phai hien ngay thang commit; nen chup cac commit
   som nhat va moi nhat trong cung repo.

4. 05-member-<ten>-week-<so>.png
   Chup lich su commit theo tung tuan va tung thanh vien. Dam bao co it nhat
   2 commit moi tuan cho moi thanh vien trong khoang thoi gian du an. Neu nhieu
   tuan, tao mot anh cho moi thanh vien moi tuan hoac mot anh tong hop doc ro
   duoc ten tac gia va thoi diem commit.

5. 06-pull-requests.png va 07-issues-project.png (neu co)
   Dung de bo sung bang chung phan chia cong viec, trao doi, review va phoi hop.
   Day la anh ho tro; chung khong thay the yeu cau ve thoi gian va tan suat commit.

LUU Y KHI CHUP

- De hien ten tai khoan/tac gia, ngay thang va repo tren anh; khong cat mat thong
  tin can de doi chieu.
- Dung mot cach nhan dien thanh vien nhat quan giua Contributors va commit log.
- Khong sua noi dung, ngay thang hay so lieu tren anh.
- Neu lich su thuc te chua du 1 thang hoac co thanh vien chua dat 2 commit/tuan,
  khong tuyen bo da dat. Bao lai tinh trang that va hoi giang vien cach xu ly.
- Neu co commit bang email khac nhau, lien ket email voi tai khoan GitHub/GitLab
  truoc khi chup Contributors de dong gop duoc tinh dung.

TRANG THAI HIEN TAI

Thu muc nay chua co anh minh chung. Hay chup truc tiep tu repo GitHub/GitLab cua
nhom va dat theo ten goi y o tren. Khong nop rieng file HUONG-DAN.txt de thay
cho cac anh lich su that.
